Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

This policy explains how Pumpfiat LLC (“Pumpfiat”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information when you use pumpfiat.com, contact our team, or participate in a Pumpfiat referral programme. For privacy questions or requests, email sales@pumpfiat.com.

Information we collect

  • Enquiries: information you submit through a contact or audit request form, such as your name, work email, company, website, and the details you choose to provide. Our onboarding form uses FormSubmit to deliver enquiries to our team; that provider processes information under its own terms.
  • Referral accounts: first and last name, registration email, referral code, referral relationship, registration source, and account timestamps.
  • Affiliate programme details: Gumroad affiliate email, profile URL, and application or verification status that you provide or that an administrator records.
  • Programme administration: sales-report fields uploaded by an administrator, which may include purchase identifiers, product, date, amount, currency, affiliate email, refund or dispute status, and payment/reward records.
  • Security: the server processes a connection IP address in memory to rate-limit registration attempts. The application does not save that rate-limit value in the participant database.

How we use information

We use information to respond to requests, create and maintain referral accounts, attribute eligible direct referrals, administer programme rewards, prevent abuse, secure the service, and meet legal or accounting obligations. We do not use referral details to create a multi-level rewards relationship.

Referral cookies and your choice

With your permission, the referral programme stores a signed, first-touch referral code in an HttpOnly cookie for up to 30 days. It is used to attribute a later registration to the person who referred you. It is not an advertising or analytics cookie. You can decline or withdraw this optional cookie; if you decline, attribution may only be available while the referral code remains on the registration page. See our Cookie Notice.

When you sign in to the administrator area, a separate HttpOnly session cookie is used to keep that session authenticated. It lasts up to eight hours and is only set after a successful login.

Sharing and storage

We share information with service providers that help deliver the website, process form submissions, or administer the referral programme, and where disclosure is required to comply with law or protect the service. Referral and programme records are stored in the application’s SQLite database. We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed in a country other than the one where you live, depending on the hosting and form-delivery services used. Contact us if you need more information about a particular request.

Retention and your rights

We retain information for as long as it is needed to respond to an enquiry, operate the relevant account or programme, keep required business records, resolve disputes, and protect the service. You may request access, correction, or deletion by emailing sales@pumpfiat.com. We will review the request and explain any information we must keep for legal, accounting, security, or programme-integrity reasons.

Security and changes

We use access controls and application safeguards appropriate to the information handled, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
