Why Pumpfiat

Built to turn reach into responsible growth

Yusuf previously worked at Hugo Technologies (Chicago, IL), where he built scalable AI-powered scraping infrastructure and ML-driven enrichment systems used by teams at Meta, Google, and Tesla.

As a Notion Partner, he has led organizational automation projects for global teams, producing $500k+ in Gumroad revenue via templates, systems and advanced workflow tools.

Pumpfiat was born from a simple but expensive problem: reliably reaching customers. Everything evolved from solving that one issue.

Viral attention is easy to create; auditable revenue is hard. Pumpfiat was built around the harder problem: helping real businesses grow through permission-based, data-verified outreach.

The name began over coffee, inspired by the momentum of pump.fun and the idea of pumping real financial value. The domain was not for sale, but after a long negotiation and an undisclosed fee, we acquired it. That investment reflects why compliance and domain reputation matter: trust should be built into growth from day one.

— “If we help businesses grow, the brand itself should represent growth on day one.”

Today, Pumpfiat manages a permission-first database of over 7 million verified prospects, backed with full audit-trail consent logs.
