2 The Three Authentication Pillars (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)

Deliverability starts with the core trio.

A. SPF — Proving Your Sending Sources

SPF tells mailbox providers which servers are allowed to send on your domain’s behalf.

Key rules:

  • Include only approved vendors
  • Remove deprecated sources
  • Avoid “SPF flattening services”
  • Maintain under 10-lookup limit

Pumpfiat always deploys strict SPF modes to prevent spoofing and shadow sends.

B. DKIM — Validating Message Integrity

DKIM cryptographically signs every outgoing email.

Prevents:

  • content tampering
  • domain impersonation
  • phishing classification

Our DKIM keys use 2048-bit encryption — the modern security minimum.

C. DMARC — Enforcing Policy & Preventing Abuse

DMARC instructs ISPs how to treat unauthorized messages.

Stages:

  • none (monitoring)
  • quarantine (spam)
  • reject (block)

For Pumpfiat, DMARC is standard — with reporting routed into automated analytics.

The authentication pipeline that protects your sender reputation

3 Google & Yahoo’s New Rules (2024 Update)

Both companies now enforce:

  • Mandatory SPF, DKIM, DMARC
  • One-click unsubscribe in the header
  • Complaint rates < 0.3%
  • Valid reverse DNS & matching HELO
  • Enforced authentication alignment
  • Clear sender identity

If any of these fail, your domain is treated as non-compliant — and inbox placement collapses.

This is why Pumpfiat builds layered authentication setups for every sending domain.

4 Domain Warm-Up Isn’t Optional Anymore

New domains have zero sending reputation.
Mailbox providers distrust them by default.

Warm-up is the process of:

  • gradually increasing volume
  • establishing trust
  • spreading sending across time zones
  • reducing complaints
  • maximizing positive engagements

Pumpfiat uses AI-driven warm-up systems to:

  • simulate natural engagement
  • throttle based on mailbox-provider feedback
  • measure hourly reputation changes
  • adapt volume based on complaint signals

This replaces outdated static warm-up schedules.

From 0 to 50K sends — reputation-safe scaling

5 Infrastructure Segmentation: The New Deliverability Superpower

High-performing teams segment infrastructure into:

  • Primary domain (brand)
  • Subdomain for cold outreach
  • Additional subdomains for testing & sequencing
  • Unique routing for each region (US/EU/APAC)
  • Dedicated IPs for high-volume lanes
  • Rotating IP pools for outreach scalability

Why this matters:
One bad campaign shouldn't make your entire domain untrustworthy.

Pumpfiat deploys domain clusters so clients can scale outreach safely.

Pumpfiat solves this from the root:

  • our database is permission-first

  • multi-source verification removes duplicates

This keeps clients in compliance and preserves reputation.

7 Content Matters — But Not the Way You Think
