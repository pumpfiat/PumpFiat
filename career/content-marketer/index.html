Content Marketer

Location: Kansas City, MO (Remote-First) Type: Full-Time Level: Mid-Level

Join Pumpfiat as a Content Marketer and educate the market on ethical, compliant outreach. You’ll create high-impact case studies, playbooks, blog posts, and thought leadership that positions Pumpfiat as the authority in permission-based growth.

About the Role

You’ll own our content strategy and production — turning client success stories, deliverability insights, and compliance expertise into compelling narratives that attract founders, growth leads, and marketing teams.

This is a remote-first role with a preference for candidates able to collaborate effectively with the Kansas City, MO timezone.

Key Responsibilities

  • • Write in-depth case studies, deliverability playbooks, and educational guides.
  • • Produce blog posts, newsletters, and social threads that drive organic traffic and authority.
  • • Collaborate with clients and customer success to uncover and tell powerful success stories.
  • • Develop thought leadership content on compliance, deliverability, and ethical growth marketing.
  • • Optimize content for SEO and distribution across LinkedIn, Twitter/X, newsletters, and partnerships.
  • • Measure content performance and iterate based on engagement, leads, and conversions.
  • • Support sales and marketing with battle cards, email templates, and pitch content.

What We’re Looking For

  • • 3+ years of content marketing or editorial experience, ideally in B2B SaaS or marketing tech.
  • • Exceptional writing and storytelling skills — clear, persuasive, and audience-focused.
  • • Portfolio of published case studies, blog posts, or long-form content that drove results.
  • • Understanding of SEO fundamentals and content distribution strategies.
  • • Experience interviewing customers and turning insights into compelling narratives.
  • • Comfortable with data — you can interpret metrics and client results to inform content.
  • • Passion for ethical marketing, compliance, and helping businesses grow sustainably.

Nice to Have

  • • Experience in email marketing, deliverability, or data privacy topics.
  • • Design or video editing skills (Canva, Figma, Premiere).
  • • Established audience or personal brand on LinkedIn/Twitter.
  • • Experience with content tools (Webflow, Ghost, Notion, Ahrefs).
