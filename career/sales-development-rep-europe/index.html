Sales Development Representative – Europe

Location: Candidates based in European countries (Remote) Type: Full-Time Level: Entry to Mid

Join Pumpfiat as a Sales Development Representative and help bring ethical, permission-based outreach to fast-growing businesses across Europe. We are looking for candidates based in European countries to strengthen our understanding of regional markets and drive outreach.

Apply Now
← Back to all PumpFiat careers

About the Role

You’ll be on the front lines of Pumpfiat’s growth, prospecting and qualifying leads through outbound email and LinkedIn outreach. Your mission is to book demos for high-value clients while demonstrating the power of compliant, high-performance campaigns.

Why Europe matters to us: We want to build authentic relationships with businesses across European countries, understand local market nuances, and tailor our outreach effectively.

This is a fully remote role for candidates based in European countries.

Key Responsibilities

  • • Research and identify high-potential prospects using Pumpfiat datasets and tools.
  • • Craft personalized outbound sequences (email + LinkedIn) that drive replies and bookings.
  • • Qualify inbound leads and schedule demos for the sales team.
  • • Meet and exceed monthly targets for qualified opportunities and pipeline generated.
  • • Collaborate with marketing to test new messaging and channels.
  • • Contribute local market insights from European markets to refine targeting and messaging.

What We’re Looking For

  • • 1–3 years of SDR, sales, or outbound prospecting experience (SaaS/tech preferred).
  • • Proven ability to write compelling cold emails and LinkedIn messages.
  • • Comfortable with tools like LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Apollo, or similar.
  • • Strong research skills and attention to personalization.
  • • Resilience, coachability, and a metrics-driven mindset.
  • • Excellent written and spoken English.
  • • Based in a European country.

Nice to Have

  • • Experience selling into or working within European startup ecosystems.
  • • Fluency in additional languages used in European markets.
  • • Track record of over-achieving quota.
