Cookie Notice

Effective date: November 26, 2026

This notice explains the cookies Pumpfiat LLC uses on pumpfiat.com. You can change your choice by clearing the site’s cookie preference and revisiting a page with a referral link, or by contacting sales@pumpfiat.com.

Optional referral attribution

If you accept referral tracking, we store a signed first-touch referral code in an HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax cookie for up to 30 days. It helps connect a later registration to the person who referred you. We do not use this cookie for advertising or analytics. You may continue without it; referral attribution may then be limited to a registration completed while the referral code is still available on the page.

Essential cookies

A preference cookie records whether you accepted or declined referral tracking for up to six months. If you sign in to the administrator area, an HttpOnly session cookie is set after login and expires after up to eight hours. These cookies support a requested preference or authenticated session.

Third-party services

Links to Gumroad and form delivery through FormSubmit take you to or use third-party services that may apply their own cookies and privacy practices. This notice does not control cookies set by those services.
