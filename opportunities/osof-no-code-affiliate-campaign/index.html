Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you join.

Does Pumpfiat registration make me a Gumroad affiliate?

No. Pumpfiat registration creates your referral profile and unique referral link. You must separately apply through the official Osof No Code Gumroad affiliate page and be approved.

When do I earn the $50 referral reward?

You earn $50 once for each unique person you directly refer who is verified and approved as an Osof No Code affiliate. Each eligible approved referral also gives you one competition entry. A registration or pending application alone does not qualify, and no product sale is required.

Do I earn from people referred by my referrals?

No. Referral rewards are paid only to the direct referrer. There are no multi-level referral commissions in this programme.

Can I request more than the standard 20% affiliate rate?

Yes. Contact sales@pumpfiat.com to discuss an affiliate percentage above 20%.

How do competition entries work?

Each eligible, verified and approved direct referral gives you one entry. More eligible approved referrals mean more entries and a better chance to win. See the official campaign rules for prize and timing details.

Who pays the product commission and referral reward?

Gumroad handles your product affiliate commission under its programme terms. Pumpfiat administers the separate $50 direct referral reward and competition entries after an eligible affiliate approval is verified.

Terms and participation conditions

Participation is subject to Osof No Code and Gumroad programme terms, local eligibility rules, and Gumroad affiliate approval. The standard Gumroad affiliate commission is 20%; contact sales@pumpfiat.com to discuss a higher percentage. Separately, each unique person you directly refer must be verified and approved as an Osof No Code affiliate for you to receive one $50 reward and one competition entry. Registrations, pending applications and indirect referrals do not qualify. Product promotion and sales are not required for this referral reward. Rewards and entries are subject to verification and the official campaign rules; no payment timing or competition outcome is guaranteed.
