Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you refer.

Does a registration or pending application qualify?

No. Your direct referral must be a unique person whose Osof No Code affiliate application is verified and approved. A Pumpfiat registration or pending Gumroad application alone earns nothing.

Do I need to promote products or generate sales?

No. You earn the one-time $50 referral reward and one competition entry when your eligible direct referral is approved. Neither your sales nor the referred affiliate's product sales are required.

Do indirect referrals count?

No. Only people directly referred through your unique Pumpfiat link can qualify for your reward and competition entry. There are no multi-level referral rewards.

How do competition entries work?

Each eligible, verified and approved direct referral gives you one entry. More eligible approved referrals mean more entries and a better chance to win. Prize and timing details are set out in the official campaign rules.

How does a referred person apply?

They register through your Pumpfiat referral link, then apply separately through the official Osof No Code Gumroad affiliate page. Their affiliate application must be approved and verified.

Eligibility and campaign terms

Eligible locations: Worldwide. Each unique person you directly refer must be verified and approved as an Osof No Code affiliate to qualify. You receive one $50 reward and one competition entry for that eligible approval. Registrations, pending applications, duplicate or self-referrals, and indirect referrals do not qualify. Product promotion and sales are not required. Rewards and entries are subject to verification and the official campaign rules; no payment timing or competition outcome is guaranteed.

Each eligible approval is counted once. Competition prize and timing details are governed by the official campaign rules.
