Anti-Spam & Ethical Use Policy

Pumpfiat provides verified, permission-based prospect data intended for compliant, ethical outreach. To protect sender reputation and ensure lawful use, all customers must adhere to the following policy.

1. Permitted Use

Clients may use Pumpfiat datasets exclusively for:

  • Legitimate business outreach
  • Segmented or personalized campaigns
  • Deliverability-safe warm-up sequences

2. Prohibited Activities

  • Mass unsolicited blasts without warm-up
  • Reselling or redistributing the dataset
  • Scraping, harvesting, or enriching using illegal methods
  • Sending misleading, fraudulent, or harmful content
  • Bypassing unsubscribe or opt-out requirements

3. Compliance Requirements

All outreach must follow applicable laws and regulations:

  • GDPR (EU)
  • CCPA (California)
  • CAN-SPAM Act (United States)
  • Local anti-spam & privacy regulations

4. Enforcement

Violations may result in immediate suspension of access without refund to protect dataset quality and compliance readiness.
