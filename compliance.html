Data Compliance & Security

Pumpfiat provides only first-party, permission-based data. Every prospect in our dataset has explicitly opted-in via one of our verified partner networks. This page summarizes our sourcing, consent standards, and security controls.

1. First-Party Data Sourcing

All records originate from fully permissioned first-party partners, including:

  • Premium B2B publishers with explicit third-party consent options
  • Industry exchanges & membership networks with authenticated user accounts
  • Opt-in comparison platforms across finance, software, HR and more
  • Verified product review ecosystems where users select “Yes — I agree to be contacted”

No scraped, harvested, or inferred email data is ever used. All partners undergo a quarterly compliance review.

2. Consent & Legal Framework

Each record includes:

  • Timestamp of opt-in
  • Consent method (checkbox, policy acceptance, or authenticated portal)
  • Allowed contact categories
  • Partner source + compliance reference ID

Our data complies with GDPR, CCPA, and CAN-SPAM requirements. Consent metadata is available in audit exports.

3. Security & Certifications

SOC 2 Type II Certification
Certification target completed — Q3 2025.

Our systems follow SOC-aligned best practices including access controls, encryption at rest and in transit, intrusion monitoring, and data retention minimization.

4. Transparency & Auditability

Every customer receives a full audit export containing:

  • Consent metadata
  • Partner source information
  • Segmentation logic used
  • Deliverability readiness diagnostics
